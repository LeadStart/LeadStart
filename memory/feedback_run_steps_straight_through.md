On 2026-09-30 I had suggested, in the Oregon brief's pace answer, holding the TuBe scan until about a week before those firms would reach Email 1 (for "fresher AI answers"). The owner's "Go." covered the whole brief, so I paused the run and told them the scan was "due around Oct 13". The owner's reaction: "Wtf why Oct 13...no, scan it now why the fuck would you tell me when it's 'Due'?"

**Why:** the owner wants batches finished once started. A wait they never asked for looks like stalling, and a "due date" makes them manage my schedule. A suggestion tucked into a 10-answer brief isn't a real decision by the owner, even when they say "Go" to the whole brief.

**How to apply:**
- Do each step as soon as its gate is approved. Ask for the next gate right away. If possible, ask for it in advance (e.g. ask for the export-download go while the scan runs).
- Don't put timing holds in suggested defaults. If timing really matters, ask it as its own plain question with the trade-off, never as a buried default.
- Report queue and start dates as facts, never as deadlines for the owner.
