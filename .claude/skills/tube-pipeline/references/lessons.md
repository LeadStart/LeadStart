# Lessons: what went wrong, and what catches it now

Read this before loosening or removing any check. Each entry is a real incident from the September 2026 WA batches.

| # | What happened | What catches it now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wrong questions (2026-09-10).** 64 of 132 firms were asked the wrong question: the wrong city or state, "lawyers" with no practice, or a category label ("Estate Planning"). | `buildTubeHandoff` writes the exact question with a locked city and state. Step 4 re-checks its shape; step 6 checks the question asked against our sheet and the report page. |
| 2 | **Missed self-mentions (2026-09-25).** A business-law firm (named #2) and an immigration firm (#4) were about to be told they "never came up": Google had used names the scan didn't recognise. | The `aliases` column carries every name the firm goes by. TuBe's export check holds these rows against the stored 3,000-character answer. Step 6 checks the report page's list and excerpt, including the owner's surname. The report page shows only ~520 characters, so it can't see a mention as deep as the immigration firm's: both checks are needed. |
| 3 | **`competitor_1` was not Google's #1.** It came from a count-then-alphabetical sort, and matched the real first pick in 1 of 21 rows. | TuBe now takes answer order and requires `top_pick_confident`. Step 6 compares it with the report's first recommended firm. |
| 4 | **A ranking site as a "competitor"** ("Best Law Firms"). | TuBe's blocklist, plus step 6's directory pattern. |
| 5 | **Names that read wrong in an email:** legal endings ("…, Attorney at Law, P.S."), ALL CAPS ("DANA REED LAW FIRM"), keyword listings ("Seattle Divorce Lawyers - Hartwell Law"), city taglines, pipes. | TuBe's `displayName()` (since `2c3b3e3`). Step 6 and the render check hold any leftover. Step 4 flags what no cleaner can fix: a listing with no real firm name ("FAMILY LAW COMPLEX LITIGATION ADVOCACY"). A person has to look at those. |
| 6 | **Stuck scans (2026-09-25).** 6 rows sat on "Running" for 9+ hours and blocked the export. The jobs died, but nothing cleared the orphaned rows. | TuBe limits each firm to 10 minutes and each PDF to 60 seconds. The worker marks a row "Running" over 30 minutes as Error; there's a Stop button; exports work on partial batches. Render auto-deploys the worker on push: the clean-up ran 63 s after its push. The earlier read that "the worker never restarted" was wrong. |
| 7 | **Scrap.io locked the account (2026-09-26)** after ~1,800 "free" count searches sized whole states, sent by throwaway scripts that called Scrap.io directly. Support had to unlock it. | Hard ceilings (2026-09-29). Every search claims a slot in LeadStart's `scrapio_search_log` first (migration 00135, shared by this skill, the app and every computer): at most 150 in 24 hours, 400 in 7 days and 1,000 in 30 days, plus 100 per script run. The throwaway scripts were removed. Ask the owner before any bulk job against an outside service; stop on the first 403/429 (`StopError`). |
| 8 | **False "same firm" matches (first test of this skill).** Five firms were dropped because their gmail.com or yahoo.com addresses matched other firms' Gmail addresses. | `FREE_MAIL` in `lib.mjs`: two firms on a personal mail provider are not one firm. |
| 9 | **A check that hard-coded copy** raised 224 false problems after the owner edited subjects in the app. | The owner edits copy live: `render-check.mts` checks values, tokens, names, scores, the opt-out and the signature, never wording. Never re-apply old drafts over the live copy. |
| 10 | **Hidden wording variation.** Spintax was left in follow-ups; the owner removed it: "we're not doing A/B testing on either of those 2 or 3". | Nothing here adds or edits copy. A/B lives only where the owner put it (Email 1's subject). |
| 11 | **Claims that weren't true for everyone.** "Some" firms have a location audit; the "6% → 45%" stat compared two different survey questions. | The owner's no-"some" rule: every line must hold for every recipient. Report exact counts. The render check blocks blank values. |
| 12 | **Re-run vs re-download.** A re-scan gets fresh AI answers that can contradict what the emails already said. | Hot leads get their existing `report_link` (it never expires). Step 4 never re-scans a firm TuBe already scanned. |
| 13 | **The page returns ~1,000 characters per read**, which silently cut lists off. | `tube-check.js` returns hex bitmasks; bulk data leaves TuBe only through the export zip, downloaded with the owner's OK. |
| 14 | **The export zip needs the owner's OK** each time: it's a file download. | Step 6's gate. |
| 15 | **Old export shapes.** Exports before 2026-09-26 lack the `firm` column. | `validate-export.mjs` refuses any export missing a contract column, rather than guessing. |
| 16 | **Reports without PDFs.** On 2026-09-25 TuBe's PDF renderer failed for 13 minutes (20:21–20:34 UTC). 9 WA firms finished with a report page but no PDF, and TuBe's download button offers them only a re-scan. | `tube-check.js` counts them (`nopdf`) and step 4 prints the count. Answer those firms' hot leads with the report link, not a re-scan. |
| 17 | **Wrong person, right firm (WA-10, 2026-09-29).** 7 of 174 export rows would have greeted the owner at a colleague's or assistant's address: "Hey Maria," to tom@…, "Hey Alan," to bkoh@…, "Hey Dana," to lee.morgan@…. | `emailPerson()` in `lib.mjs` (nicknames, initials, firm mailboxes). Step 4 flags the rows, and step 6 holds them. |
| 18 | **Names TuBe's cleaner still misses.** "Hartley Law Accident and Injury Lawyers" and "Brandt Okafor Mesothelioma & Sex Abuse Attorneys" were printed into subject lines, as were titles like "Dana Whitfield, Attorney". 14 of 174 on WA-10. | `KEYWORD_TAIL` and `TITLE_TAIL` in `lib.mjs` hold them at step 6. Brand names that end in "Attorneys" ("Sam Ortiz Attorneys") still pass. The lasting fix is in TuBe's `displayName()`. |
| 19 | **A national firm under a local website.** "Practus" (a national virtual firm) was the Google listing for a solo lawyer's local website. | Held by hand with `--exclude`. National firm names belong in `LARGE_FIRM` in `src/lib/tube/handoff.ts`. |
