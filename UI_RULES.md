## The doctrine

A pale page under a faint brand bloom. Surfaces are white cards with a **soft edge and a soft shadow**, rounder than before (cards 16px, popups 24px). **Every control is a pill**: buttons, fields, selects, segmented tabs, chips. Filled controls carry Forge's **140° sheen** (a lighter top left, a deeper foot) and **no shadow** (Forge's flat-buttons rule). Labels above lists and groups are small uppercase eyebrows. Popups sit on a blurred dim and, on a phone, are sheets from the bottom that pull shut. Things that appear grow in over 220ms and go a little quicker (160ms); sheets rise on iOS's curve (520ms up, 420ms down).

## Tokens (`src/app/globals.css` `:root`)

- **Page and surfaces:** `--background #f4f6fb` under two radial blooms (`--bloom` brand at 7%, top right; `--bloom-2` green at 3.5%, foot). `--card` / `--popover #fff`, `--secondary #f8fafc`, `--muted #f1f5f9`.
- **Edges:** `--border #e2e8f0` (fields, outline buttons, popups), `--border-soft #e9eef5` (cards, dividers inside cards).
- **Text:** `--foreground #0f172a`, `--secondary-foreground #334155`, `--muted-foreground #64748b`.
- **Brand:** `--primary` / `--ring` `#2E37FE`. Sidebar tokens unchanged (navy).
- **Sheens** (use as `bg-(image:--tex-…)` over the matching solid fill): `--tex-brand` (140°, `#3b46fe → #2931ec`), `--tex-white` (`#fff → #f1f5f9`), `--tex-ink`, `--tex-danger`.
- **Shadows** (utilities `shadow-card`, `shadow-float`, `shadow-pop`): `--ls-shadow-card` for cards at rest, `--ls-shadow-float` for a lifted card or hover, `--ls-shadow-pop` for menus and popups.
- **Radius:** `--radius: 0.75rem`, so `rounded-xl` lands at 16px on cards.
- **Motion:** `--ease-out-soft cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)` (grow in), `--ease-sheet cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1)` (sheets).

## What it forbids (each is a debt grep in `STYLE_SWEEP.md`)

- **Hex that duplicates a token** in class strings or inline styles (`#2E37FE`, `#0F172A`, `#64748B`, `#E2E8F0`, `#EDEEFF` …): use `primary`, `foreground`, `muted-foreground`, `border`, `brand-50` … Exemptions: other brands' colours (LinkedIn `#0A66C2`, Google `#EA4335`), the rail's own navy text tints, chart and series colour data (SVG needs the value), email HTML.
- **An inline brand background on a shared `<Button>`** (`style={{ background: '#2E37FE' }}`): it hides the sheen. The default variant already paints it.
- **Hand-built overlays** (`fixed inset-0` with their own backdrop, Esc handler or scroll lock): use the shared `Dialog` / `AlertDialog`, which carry every way out. A loading screen with nothing to close uses `LoadingVeil`.
- **Hand-built menus and dropdowns** without the menu chrome: use `DropdownMenu`, or `ls-menu-in rounded-2xl border border-border bg-popover shadow-pop`.
- **A native `<select>` without `ls-select`** (`ls-select-sm` for small ones).
- **Gradients** other than the `--tex-*` sheens, the page bloom, the rail, the phone pill and the loading veil.
- **`shadow-sm/md/lg/xl` on surfaces:** use `shadow-card`, `shadow-float` or `shadow-pop`. (A switch knob may keep its `shadow-sm`.)
- **Card overrides that undo the card** (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-none`, `border-border/50` on a `Card`): let `Card` be. The one exception: a panel inside a popup drops its frame (`border-0 py-0 shadow-none`), because the popup is the frame (Integrations).
- **`!important`**, except: phone fields' 16px text (iOS zoom), the phone sheet's full width and padding (it must beat a call site's `max-w-*`), and reduced motion.

## Component targets

- **Card** (`ui/card.tsx`): `rounded-xl` (16px), `border-border-soft`, `bg-card`, `shadow-card`, `py-5`, header and content `px-5`. CardTitle 15px semibold; CardDescription 13px. Footer `bg-secondary` over a soft edge.
- **Button** (`ui/button.tsx`): a pill (`rounded-full`), 38px default (`sm` 30, `xs` 26, `lg` 42), 13.5px semibold. `default` = brand fill + `--tex-brand`; `outline` / `secondary` = white + `--tex-white` + hairline; `ghost` = a quiet tint on hover; `destructive` = red + `--tex-danger`. No shadow. A press dims slightly and scales to 0.985. `.btn-gold` / `.btn-blue` are the brand pill for straggler call sites.
- **Fields:** `Input` a 40px pill, `Textarea` 12px corners, both `border-input`, white, a faint lift, focus = `border-primary` + a 3px brand glow at 15%. 16px text on phones, 13.5px from `md`. Native selects: `ls-select` (38px pill, chevron, same glow); `SelectTrigger` matches.
- **Tabs** (`ui/tabs.tsx`): the default variant is Forge's **pill segmented control** (muted track, soft edge, the active segment white with `--tex-white`). A tab row too wide for a phone scrolls inside itself (`overflow-x-auto`, no scrollbar), never the page. The `line` variant (`ui/underline-tab.tsx`) keeps its underline: `<TabsList variant="line" className="h-auto w-full justify-start gap-6 rounded-none border-b border-border p-0">`.
- **Badges:** pill chips; the six `badge-*` fills stay solid (`green #059669 · amber #d97706 · red #dc2626 · blue #2E37FE · purple #7c3aed · slate #475569`). Soft brand chips are `border-transparent bg-brand-50 text-brand-500`.
- **Menus** (DropdownMenu, Select popup, `ls-menu`): 16px corners, `shadow-pop`, uppercase group labels. They grow out of their trigger over 220ms and close over 160ms, turning round mid-way.
- **Popups** (`Dialog`, `AlertDialog` via `.ls-dim` + `.ls-pop`; `ui/sheet.tsx` is not used anywhere and was left as it was): dim `rgba(15,23,42,.32)` with a 3px blur; the popup 24px corners, `shadow-pop`, no default max width (call sites set `sm:max-w-*`). Desktop: grows from 0.96 and fades over 180ms, leaves over 160ms. Phone (< 640px): a **bottom sheet** with a working grabber, rising 520ms and falling 420ms on `--ease-sheet`, pulled shut past a third of its height or by a flick (`lib/ui/sheet-pull.ts`, `use-sheet-pull.ts`); a short pull springs back; content that scrolls scrolls first. Every popup closes by its X, a tap or click outside and Esc, and stays on the page until its way out has finished (Base UI `onOpenChangeComplete`). These rules live in `@layer components`, so a call site's utilities still win.
  - **With the keyboard** (`lib/ui/sheet-keyboard.ts`, a port of Forge's `useSheetKeyboard` for Safari): on a touch screen a popup opens with the cursor on itself, never in a box no finger tapped. A tapped box (or its label, or a tap just beside it) takes the cursor with `preventScroll`, and so does a box the keyboard's arrows move to, so iOS never slides the page; a slide iOS makes anyway is put back. The sheet then stands on the keyboard: met at that keyboard's last height (kept per kind of keyboard and orientation) as it rises, corrected when Safari reports it, foot and height gliding together (up 240ms `ease`, down 200ms `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`), and the box scrolled into view in its own scroller. As the keyboard goes, the sheet's foot drops behind it at once and only its top glides. Closed with the keyboard up (pull, grabber, X, Cancel), it keeps its place, waits out iOS's 140ms hold in drawing, then slides off from there.
- **Loading veil** (`layout/loading-veil.tsx`, `.ls-veil`): a full-screen page bloom with the bounce loader, fading in over 160ms and out over 220ms. Used for signing in and the post-login load.
- **Table** (`ui/table.tsx`): eyebrow header, hairline rows, a hover tint; never wrapped in a Card.
- **Sidebar** (`layout/sidebar.tsx`, `.app-rail`): the floating navy rail is unchanged in colour and geometry (fixed, 24px inset, 200px, 20px corners, its float shadow). Header = Forge's brand row: the mark on a 34px white tile with a soft bloom behind it, beside the spaced `LEADSTART` wordmark. Rows are 13.5px, rounded, a quiet `bg-white/7` hover; the chosen row is the brand fill with `--tex-brand` and a soft drop. Section labels are uppercase eyebrows. Below 1024px it is the slide-in drawer.

## Interaction rules (carry these; they cost nothing)

No Card wrapper around a table or stat grid; filled-by-role button pairs; no `+` prefix on create buttons; toasts only on explicit save; 25/page URL-backed pagination; whole-row click-through on lists; a circular back arrow left of the title on every non-top-level surface; omit fabricated trend arrows when there's no prior-period series. **Shift or Caps Lock alone never lights a focus ring** (`lib/ui/press-focus.ts` marks the page `data-focus="press"` on a press; Tab brings the rings back). **Every popup closes the same smooth way** (above). **No sideways scroll**: every page measures `scrollWidth === clientWidth` at 1440, 1024, 390 and 375.

### The flat contract (2026-08-22 to 2026-10-26), replaced by task #228

Kept as it stood, for reference. Its structure rules for the Settings hub, Integrations grid, Back navigation, Calculator pages and the Overview still hold; its looks (hairline-only cards, 8px controls, no shadows, solid fills) were replaced by the contract above.

#### The doctrine

Depth comes from a **1px hairline border + a surface tint**, never gradients, glows, or stacked shadows. **One shadow tier**, reserved for overlays (Dialog/Popover/DropdownMenu/Toast) — flat surfaces carry a hairline, not a shadow. 4px spacing grid (Tailwind default). Radius **8px controls / 12px base cards / full badges** (`--radius: 0.5rem`); dashboard stat + roster cards go rounder (`rounded-2xl` rows ≈14px, feature cards ≈20px) — see **Stat / KPI cards**.

#### Tokens (defined in `src/app/globals.css` `:root`)

`--primary` stays LeadStart blue `#2E37FE`. Neutrals are slate (`--background #f8fafc`, `--card #fff`, `--muted #f1f5f9`, `--border #e2e8f0`, `--foreground #0f172a`, `--muted-foreground #64748b`). Sidebar is its own dark scale (`--sidebar #0f172a`, `--sidebar-foreground #cbd5e1`, `--sidebar-primary #2E37FE`, `--sidebar-primary-foreground #fff`, `--sidebar-border #1e293b`). `--radius: 0.5rem`.

#### What "flat" forbids (each is a debt-grep in `STYLE_SWEEP.md` → target 0)

- Decorative gradients anywhere (backgrounds, buttons, badges, hero, table headers, login). Exemptions: functional gradients (CSS mask tricks) and the **sidebar's brand gradient** (a deliberate exception — see Component targets).
- Colored / multi-layer / inset box-shadows on surfaces; `filter: drop-shadow` glows; neumorphic stacks; page aurora/orbs; glass `backdrop-filter`. Exemptions: the floating nav rail's single elevation shadow + its logo bloom (deliberate — see Sidebar), and the **mobile floating nav pill's Liquid-Glass material** (backdrop blur + specular + elevation), scoped to that one `< lg` chrome surface (deliberate — see Mobile nav).
- `!important` override blocks keyed on `data-slot` — style the primitive directly instead.
- Inline `#hex` chips and `bg-[#hex]` arbitraries — use `bg-primary` / token classes. (Deferred-cleanup while dark mode is out of scope, but new code must use tokens.)
- More than one shadow tier.

#### Component targets

Flex `items-center` lands the circle on the title’s cap-height band (measured 0.01px off at 24px/32px Poppins), which reads right for a title with no descender ("Team") but ~3.5px high for the many with one ("Tags", "Billing", "Campaigns"). 2px closes most of that gap while staying imperceptible on the rest; a per-title correction is banned because the arrow would then jump between pages. #### Interaction rules (carry these; they cost nothing)

No Card wrapper around a table or stat grid; filled-by-role button pairs; no `+` prefix on create buttons; toasts only on explicit save; 25/page URL-backed pagination; whole-row click-through on lists; a circular back arrow left of the title on every non-top-level surface; omit fabricated trend arrows when there's no prior-period series.
